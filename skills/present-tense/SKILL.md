- Narrate in the present tense ("she opens the door"), close to one viewpoint character per scene.
- Use past tense only for things that are truly in the past of the scene: memories and backstory, kept brief.
- Present tense makes summary feel odd; stay in scene, and cut between scenes with a break rather than narrating the time in between.
- The immediacy is the point: no foreknowledge in the narration ("she will later regret").
- In revision: hunt every verb for slips into the past tense, which are the most common error in this voice.
