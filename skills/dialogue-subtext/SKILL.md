- In every conversation, each person wants something from it. Know what, and let them pursue it indirectly.
- People rarely answer the question they were asked. They deflect, change the subject, answer a different question.
- Cut the greetings, the small talk and the goodbyes unless they carry tension.
- Give each speaker a recognisable way of talking: sentence length, favourite words, what they avoid saying. A reader should know who speaks without the tag.
- Use "said" or no tag at all; let an action beat show who speaks and how they feel.
- Never let characters tell each other what both already know for the reader's benefit.
- Break long speeches with interruptions, actions and silences.
