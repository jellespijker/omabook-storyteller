- Open each chapter in motion: a person doing something, in a place, with a want, in the first paragraph. Re-anchor whose scene it is, where and when, without a recap.
- End each chapter at a point of change, not of rest. Vary how: a revelation, a decision made, a new danger, a question raised, an arrival, a reversal of what the chapter seemed to promise.
- Not every ending is a cliffhanger. A quiet ending works when it leaves a clear, open question.
- Cut the last paragraph if it explains or sums up the turn before it.
- Keep at least one question open at the end of every chapter, and close at least one older one now and then, so the reader feels progress.
- In planning, give every chapter a "hook" line: what the reader is wondering as they turn the page.
